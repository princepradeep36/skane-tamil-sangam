Privacy Policy

How Skåne Tamil Sangam collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who we are

Skåne Tamil Sangam is a non-profit cultural association for the Tamil community in Skåne, Sweden. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at skanetamilsangam.sweden@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Event registration: name, email, phone and WhatsApp number, number of adults and children, volunteer and cultural-activity choices, comments, photo consent, payment status and the Swish payment screenshot you upload.
  • Food pre-orders (Click2Eat): name, phone number, the items you order and payment status.
  • Member accounts: name, email, membership type and a securely stored password. If you use Sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Contact form: what you write to us.

Why we use it

  • To organise our events and confirm your registration and payment.
  • To send you event information, payment confirmations and reminders by email or WhatsApp.
  • To pass your food order to the food stall you ordered from.
  • To manage your membership and let you sign in.

Who sees it

Only Skåne Tamil Sangam's organisers. Food stalls see the orders placed with them. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Our website and data are hosted on a server in the EU, and our emails are sent through Gmail (Google).

How long we keep it

Event and order data is kept for as long as needed to run and follow up the event and for our accounts. Member data is kept while you are a member. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email us at skanetamilsangam.sweden@gmail.com. You can also contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
