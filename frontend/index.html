What we offer

What We Stand For

Three simple values that guide everything we do as a community.

🤝

சமூகம் (Community)

Connecting Tamil families across Skåne through regular meetups, shared celebrations, and social gatherings that bring us closer.

🎭

கலாச்சாரம் (Culture)

Celebrating our rich heritage through grand festivals like Pongal, Deepavali, and Chithirai Vizha that honour our traditions.

📚

மொழி (Language)

Promoting Tamil language learning for the next generation through workshops and community-led immersion programs.
