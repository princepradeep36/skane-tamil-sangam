Who We Are

Skåne Tamil Sangam was established with the vision of bringing together the Tamil-speaking community in Southern Sweden. What began as small family gatherings has evolved into a vibrant organization dedicated to celebrating our linguistic and cultural identity.

We are a non-profit organization focused on preserving Tamil values while fostering a strong sense of integration and community within Swedish society.

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a platform for social interaction, cultural exchange, and mutual support among Tamils in Skåne, and to pass on our rich heritage to the next generation born on Swedish soil.

🌈

Our Vision

A thriving, inclusive, and supportive Tamil community that contributes positively to the multicultural fabric of Sweden, bridging ancient traditions with modern life.
